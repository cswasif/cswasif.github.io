selected publications

  1. arXiv
    Prefix Count Limits Can Increase First-Hit Discovery in Card Reissuance
    Wasif Faisal
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.31398, Sep 2026
    Abs Bib HTML PDF

    Identified situations in which caps on how many cards can share a number prefix can make active cards easier to find in a BIN attack (attempts to identify valid card numbers); demonstrated the effect in simulations using 50,000 synthetic candidates. The findings help banks and other card issuers assess reissuance policies for card-number discovery risk, rather than relying on count limits alone. Under review for Financial Cryptography and Data Security (FC) 2027.
