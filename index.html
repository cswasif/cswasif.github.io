Wasif Faisal

md.wasif.faisal@g.bracu.ac.bd

Early-career security researcher specializing in payment security, cyber threat intelligence, and transaction protocol analysis.

I study payment fraud and authentication weaknesses, using empirical evidence and protocol analysis to turn technical findings into practical risk recommendations. My goal is to protect digital payments, authentication pipelines, and customer transactions from emerging adversarial abuse.

The notable works include

  • pre-authorization screening oracles (arXiv’26)
  • card reissuance prefix limits (arXiv’26).

I completed all degree requirements for my B.Sc. in Computer Science at BRAC University in Summer 2026, where I led faculty-supervised payment security thesis research on Card-Not-Present (CNP) vulnerabilities (CNP-SHIELD) under Senior Lecturer Md. Akhtaruzzaman Adnan and Professor S. M. Taiabul Haque, PhD.

I serve as an Artifact Evaluation Program Committee Reviewer for the ACM Conference on Computer and Communications Security (CCS 2026-B).

Since 2018, I have conducted independent research tracking adversarial card-testing methodologies, BIN attack tools, and underground payment-fraud ecosystems.

I maintain an open interactive EMV 3DS reference, the EMV 3-D Secure Protocol Lab (website).

Best reached by email or LinkedIn for research, collaboration, and security opportunities.
