This work centers on payment authentication as a systemic security problem rather than a narrow implementation bug.

The research focuses on:

- EMV 3DS behavior across real transaction environments
- attacker-observable weaknesses and trust-boundary failures
- disciplined scope control, conservative claims, and remediation-oriented writing

The goal is to produce publishable and defensible analysis that helps payment, fraud, and product-security teams reason more clearly about protocol behavior and abuse practicality.
