This research paper investigates a subtle structural risk in payment card issuance policies:

- **Venue**: Paper under review for Financial Cryptography and Data Security (FC) 2027 ([arXiv:2609.31398](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31398)).
- **Role**: Lead author.
- **Key Insight**: Identified situations in which caps on how many cards can share a number prefix can make active cards easier to find in a BIN attack (attempts to identify valid card numbers).
- **Evaluation**: Demonstrated the effect in simulations using 50,000 synthetic candidates.
- **Impact**: The findings help banks and other card issuers assess reissuance policies for card-number discovery risk, rather than relying on count limits alone.
