This project captures the engineering-facing side of my research work: understanding how payment and authentication logic behaves in real implementations and how that behavior can be improved without exaggerated claims.

Typical focus areas include:

- hosted and merchant-hosted integration patterns
- request binding, response normalization, and flow hardening
- practical security communication for fraud, risk, and engineering stakeholders

The emphasis is not generic pentesting. It is careful analysis of transaction-system behavior and how protocol or product decisions shape abuse opportunities in practice.
