This project represents the thesis work completed for the B.Sc. in Computer Science at BRAC University:

- **Title**: *CNP-SHIELD: Investigating Card-Not-Present (CNP) Vulnerabilities in Payment Gateways*
- **Role**: Project lead and lead author
- **Supervisors**: [Md. Akhtaruzzaman Adnan](https://cse.bracu.ac.bd/faculty_profile/408/md_akhtaruzaman_adnan) (Senior Lecturer), [Prof. S. M. Taiabul Haque, PhD](https://www.bracu.ac.bd/about/people/s-m-taiabul-haque-phd) (Professor)

### Key Focus Areas

- Hosted and merchant-hosted payment gateway integration architectures
- Request binding, response normalization, and error disclosure analysis
- Card-testing (enumeration/BIN attack) risk and mitigation strategies
- Responsible disclosure and actionable control recommendations for fintech and risk teams
