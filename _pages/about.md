I am an independent security researcher and Computer Science undergraduate at BRAC University working at the intersection of payment security, fraud intelligence, and transaction-system analysis. My work focuses on card-not-present fraud ecosystems, EMV 3-D Secure protocol behavior, payment gateway security, and attacker-observable trust-boundary failures in real-world transaction systems.

I work across protocol analysis, live trace investigation, fraud tooling research, adversarial community monitoring, gateway reverse engineering, responsible disclosure, and remediation design. I am strongest when turning messy real-world behavior into defensible findings, bounded claims, and practical guidance for payment, fraud, and product-security teams.

Recent work includes the open, vendor-neutral [EMV 3-D Secure Protocol Lab](https://cnpshield.github.io/3dslab/), an interactive reference for visualizing and debugging 3DS browser and app flows across published specification versions. I also lead author a faculty-supervised payment-security research effort currently being revised for submission to a stronger security venue.
