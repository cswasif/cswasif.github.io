\section*{Research and Projects}
\textbf{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32206}{The 3DS Oracle: BIN Attack via the Network}}\hfill 2026\par
Lead author; arXiv preprint. Examines how client-visible EMV 3-D Secure responses can reveal pre-authorization card and issuer-control signals; combines standards analysis, formal modeling, and bounded evaluation to develop payment-risk and mitigation recommendations.\par
\vspace{2pt}
\textbf{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31398}{Prefix Count Limits Can Increase First-Hit Discovery in Card Reissuance}}\hfill 2026\par
Lead author; arXiv preprint (arXiv:2609.31398; under review). Identified situations in which caps on how many cards can share a number prefix can make active cards easier to find in a BIN attack (attempts to identify valid card numbers); demonstrated the effect in simulations using 50,000 synthetic candidates. The findings help banks and other card issuers assess reissuance policies for card-number discovery risk, rather than relying on count limits alone.\par
\vspace{2pt}
\textbf{\href{https://cnpshield.github.io/3dslab/}{EMV 3-D Secure Protocol Lab}}\hfill Public research and education resource\par
Built a public, vendor-neutral reference site for comparing 3-D Secure participants, messages, and decision points across published specification versions.\par
